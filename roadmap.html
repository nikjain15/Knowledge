Roadmap

The order rule (J, our judgement; no regulator sets a rollout order): knowledge before search, search before any AI consumer, staff channels before member-facing ones because risk rises as a person leaves the loop, and every consumer behind the same read contract. Search goes live in phase 2 because phase 1 is what makes its answers approved, scoped and current BR-01; D13. Durations are assumed.
