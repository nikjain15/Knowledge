Key tradeoff: the unit of knowledge

What does Legal approve, and what does a channel cite? We chose the topic: one page with locked clauses as keyed sub-units, ranked below it and cited above it (D23).

Hours per unit are assumptions for comparison, not estimates of anyone's effort. The ranking survives any multiplier, because B re-opens only the topics a change touches.

Why

The regulation locks sentences but files documents R-04, R-18. Every knowledge standard authors at the topic S-26, S-32. Retrieval does better below the topic J-12.

What it costs

Two unit types, plus an extraction step that must decide which spans are clauses, checked by the approver.

What would flip it

  • A corpus that is mostly whole standardized documents. Then option A wins.
  • A holdout where topic-level retrieval already meets the bar. Then option C, and the child index is dropped.
  • A regulator requiring document-level citation. Then the topic becomes a locator inside the document.
